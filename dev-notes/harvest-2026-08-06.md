# Harvest 2026-08-06 — mechanisms adopted from the ecosystem survey

Consumers: this build (spec), the fire-rate review (fire log +
warn-mode promotion decisions), future guard-design sessions
(deferred-ideas list, PermissionRequest note).

Survey: opus discovery dispatch 2026-08-06, session-transcript
report. Verdict: no wholesale replacement exists — the ecosystem
splits into prose-discipline-unenforced (obra/superpowers, 267k★,
zero policy hooks) and hook-enforcement-security-shaped (nothing
above 100★ inspects Agent/Task at all). Five of our lanes have no
public counterpart found (push-gate, amend-gate, skill-gate,
escalation asymmetry, discovery-volume). Operator decision:
keep the plugin, adopt six mechanisms.

## Adopted (this build, v0.4.0)

1. **Fire log** — every guard fire (deny/ask/warn/block) appends
   JSONL to `~/.local/share/claude/dispatch-guards-fires.jsonl`
   (`$CLAUDE_DISPATCH_GUARDS_FIRELOG` override). Basis: the corpus'
   fire-rate review had no instrument — fires were unattributable
   after the fact (deny-tag docstring in `_dispatch_common`).
   Source idea: hookbench (waitdeadai/hookbench) — measure the
   guards; our variant logs live fires rather than benching only.

2. **Warn-mode staging** (`guard_modes` policy key, per-guard
   `"warn"|"deny"|"off"`) — a new or suspect lane runs in warn mode
   (additionalContext "would deny" + fire-log line) before it
   denies. Basis: "a guard firing on legitimate work trains the
   override reflex" (corpus, Calibration §1); staging is the cure
   observed in charliehzm/medharness (`HOOK_MODE=warn|block`).
   New speculative lanes SHIP default-warn; promotion to deny is a
   fire-rate-review decision against the log.

3. **Replay bench** (`tools/replay-bench.py` + `tools/corpus/*.jsonl`;
   always-run consumer: the dotfiles doctor's `check_replay_bench`,
   dotfiles commit 091dea9 — a checker nobody runs decays, gap
   surfaced by the bench's own executor)
   — runs the real hook scripts over recorded/curated stdin payloads,
   classifies the outcome (deny/ask/context/block/silent), fails on
   any miss or false-fire. This IS the end-to-end deny-arm test
   (survey items 2+4 converge): the `--test` bite-tests pin function
   arms; the bench pins the process boundary (stdin→stdout JSON) and
   the historical false-fire regressions. Sources:
   woditschka/agentic-coding-reference (committed per-hook tests),
   hookbench (catch-rate/false-positive framing). Boundary: stateful
   guards (writer-claims) e2e-test inside their own `--test` (state
   seeding); the bench stays stateless.

4. **report-form-gate** (PreToolUse SendMessage, subagent only,
   default-warn) — the computable slice of schema-validating the §2
   closing report: a message carrying ≥4 distinct slot markers
   `(a)…(h)` is report-shaped; required slots a–g missing → fire
   naming them. Full prose validation is judgment-shaped and stays
   with the dispatcher (§4 booking). Basis: read-only-tail reports
   (verifier/discovery) legitimately carry no slots, and the
   SubagentStop side cannot distinguish dispatch kind → the
   marker-threshold predicate is the near-zero-false-fire slice.
   Source: agentic-coding-reference schema-validated handoffs,
   adapted to prose-message reports (§2 binding: reports travel in
   SendMessage, files are harness-blocked).

5. **writer-claims-gate** (PreToolUse+PostToolUse Write|Edit,
   default-warn) — mechanizes "one writer per working copy /
   disjoint per-file ownership" (corpus, Insurance; skill §1).
   PostToolUse records subagent write claims (file, agent_id, ts);
   PreToolUse fires when a subagent writes a file another agent
   claimed within TTL (`write_claim_ttl_hours`, default 6), and
   REMINDS (never denies) the main session when it writes a file a
   live subagent claimed (§4 mirror duty). Default-warn basis:
   stale-claim false fires are the known failure of lock schemes
   (dotagents' explicit-release lock has the converse problem);
   warn + TTL + fire-rate review before any deny promotion.
   Source: kitepon-rgb/dotagents writer-reservation (`P11_WRITER_BUSY`).
   Accepted residue: Bash-mediated writes (sed -i, tee, heredocs)
   are unseen — same outer net as push/amend gates.

6. **PermissionRequest seam** — documented in
   `permission-request-seam.md` (same directory), UNVERIFIED
   locally; carries its own verification step.

## Residue (booked 2026-08-06 from the bench executor's report)

- Six guards have NO bench coverage (brief scope): brief-reminder,
  push-claim-reminder, discovery-volume-reminder, report-enforcer,
  dispatch-log, report-reminder — their `--test` bites remain their
  only net. Extend the corpus when one of them changes.
- Corpus payloads are curated, not captured from live hook
  invocations — harness input-shape drift passes the bench while
  guards die fail-open (same residue as the bites; the doctor
  tripwire is the compensation).
- Catch-rate reads "the enumerated corpus passes", not coverage of
  the guards' real input space.

## Deferred (recorded, not built — each names why)

- **Multi-part report delivery ack** — SendMessage returning success
  did not prove arrival (observed live: two parts reported sent,
  not received, re-demanded). A dispatcher-side ack convention is
  forms.md §2 territory — anneal-dev pass, with the incident as
  evidence.

- **Status enum in the report contract** (`DONE | DONE_WITH_CONCERNS |
  BLOCKED | NEEDS_CONTEXT`, obra/superpowers implementer-prompt) —
  a forms.md §2 corpus edit; goes through anneal-dev, not this build.
- **Materialize the brief to a file, pass the path** (superpowers
  `task-brief`) — outbound payload-vs-pointer; skill §1 already
  allows brief FILES; a mechanical brief-extractor is a tools/
  candidate once a repeated need shows.
- **Bounded fix-loop with breaker** (superpowers, 5 rounds →
  escalate model → adjudicate) — dispatcher-conduct corpus material
  (skill §4), not a hook; anneal-dev territory.
- **Sanctioned-path auto-allow paired with the deny**
  (agentic-coding-reference handoff-allow) — pattern noted: where a
  deny lane bites legitimate work, the repair is a verified
  exemption; our current lanes have no sanctioned-command twin yet.
- **Budget hard-stop** (claude-swarm) — needs an orchestrator-owned
  runtime; our premise is guarding a normal session. The fable ask
  dialog is the operator-held equivalent.
